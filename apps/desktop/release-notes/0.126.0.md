- Note outline on iPhone: short lines on the right edge; tap them to open the list of sections and jump to one. On Mac, the outline lists the note's sections again.
- The iPhone sync indicator is now a small icon instead of a text pill. Tap it to see sync details.
- Fewer sync conflicts: when you edit the same note on Mac and iPhone, Kore now keeps both changes automatically, and asks you only when the same line was changed two different ways.
- iPhone: pull down from the top of Daily, Tasks or any note to search all your notes.
- Kore Cloud (preview): an early version of keeping your vault reachable by Claude, ChatGPT and other agents even when your Mac is off, running free in your own Cloudflare account. Find it in Settings → Agents → Kore Cloud.
