- Canvas boards are editable on the Mac. You can move and resize cards on the grid, edit text and group names, add notes, text cards and groups, remove with Backspace, and undo with ⌘Z.
- Whiteboard tools: pull an arrow from a card's side onto another card, and colour cards, groups and arrows.
- Text cards can become a pill, diamond, circle or parallelogram. The shapes are compatible with Obsidian's Advanced Canvas plugin.
- Canvases no longer show a page header, and panning and zooming large boards is much smoother.
- On iPhone, canvases stay view-only.
