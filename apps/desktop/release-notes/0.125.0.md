- New Timeline view for types: notes show as bars from their start date to their end date, with week, month and quarter zoom. On Mac you drag a bar to move its dates; on iPhone a tap opens the note.
- Mac: Settings opens as a large centered window over the blurred workspace. The note underneath stays where it was; Esc, a click outside or Close take you back to it.

- Typing in long notes is faster: the editor no longer redraws menus and handles on every key.
- iPhone: the "Due today" card with other notes' tasks no longer appears above today's daily.
